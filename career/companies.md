## Notes

**Strongest portfolio matches (apply first within each tier):**
- **Rivos, SiFive, Western Digital** — your RISC-V + CFI + Newlib patch combination is rare. These companies will immediately understand the value of eSC-V.
- **Crowdstrike** — bumped to Tier 2 because their Linux kernel security work (Falcon sensor is a kernel module) maps directly to your CFI, ROP/JOP, and kernel exploit research interest.
- **Seagate** — underrated. They run RISC-V in storage SoCs and hire firmware engineers. Small team means your profile stands out more than at a Google or Qualcomm.
- **Espressif** — you've shipped real projects on their hardware *and* you know RISC-V deeply (C3/C6/C2 are RISC-V). That's a genuinely rare combination for their team.
- **Arm** — Morello is Arm's own hardware capability/CFI security research project. Your Zicfilp/Smcfiss work is the RISC-V equivalent. Frame it that way.
